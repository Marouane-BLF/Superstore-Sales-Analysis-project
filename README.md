# Super Store Sales & Delivery Analytics

An interactive **Power BI and Power Query** project exploring retail sales performance and delivery operations. The goal is to turn order-level data into business-focused insights on sales trends, product categories, customer segments, regional performance, and shipping efficiency.

## Project Objectives

- Track overall sales performance and year-over-year trends.
- Compare performance across regions, categories, and customer segments.
- Explore delivery times and late-delivery patterns.
- Present key metrics in an accessible, manager-friendly dashboard.
- Practice data preparation, KPI design, and business storytelling with Power BI.

## Questions Explored

- How do sales change over time?
- Which regions contribute most to sales?
- Which product categories and customer segments perform best?
- How long do deliveries take, and how frequently are they late?
- What can sales and delivery metrics reveal about operational performance?

## Report Highlights

The dashboard is designed to explore sales, category and segment performance, regional results, and delivery efficiency. Exact KPI values and conclusions should be interpreted from the dataset and Power BI report included in this repository.

## Tools & Skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — interactive dashboard and report development
- **Power Query** — data import and preparation
- **DAX** — measures and KPI calculations, where used
- **Business intelligence** — KPI selection, visual design, and business interpretation

## Getting Started

1. Open the `dashboard/` folder and download the Power BI report when it has been uploaded.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
3. If Power BI requests a source location, point it to the dataset in `data/cleaned/` or to your local copy.
4. Refresh the data and review the model and measure definitions before using the results.

## Data & Interpretation Notes

- Keep the original dataset unchanged in `data/raw/` and place the report-ready version in `data/cleaned/`.
- Document only transformations actually performed during data preparation.
- KPI definitions (including sales growth, delivery time, and late-delivery rate) should be checked in the report before being compared with other analyses.
- Do not upload confidential, personal, or otherwise restricted data.
